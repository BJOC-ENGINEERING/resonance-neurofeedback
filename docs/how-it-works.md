# How Resonance works

1. **Signal.** Each sensor is filtered and turned into a power spectrum from a two-second window, refreshed ten times a second. Blinks, movement, muscle tension and poor contact are detected per sensor and never count toward reward.
2. **Baseline.** A short recording sets your personal 100% for every measure. Targets are percentages of that baseline, so protocols carry between people and days.
3. **Rules.** Train a measure up, keep another down, or both. Reward needs every enabled rule to pass, optionally sustained for a hold.
4. **Reward.** The flock gathers as you hold, then colours and speeds up. Chimes or a drone follow the same signal. Milestones mark accumulated rewarded time.
5. **Heart.** Muse 2 and Muse S also read your pulse. Breathe with the pacer at your resonance rate and heart rate rises and falls with each breath; coherence measures how smooth and steady that wave is.
6. **Review.** Every session logs blocks, streaks, recoveries and a reward timeline. Add notes and watch the trend. Blinded sham sessions and before-and-after check-ins let you test whether the training does anything for you.
